# Early cup winner source comparison — 11 September 2026

The independent public-source comparison covered **366 of 366 stored main-cup records in 33 countries** represented in `qa/live-cup-gap-results.json`. England, Italy, Poland, Belgium and Brazil were handled separately. The screening range is displayed local seasons up to 14, except France, Germany and Sweden up to 24. It is not a claim that every screened row precedes global season 24.

The National Trophies tables agreed exactly with 336 stored winner names. Thirty names differed. The initial public-source review found seven corroborated winner records, three likely source anomalies and twenty candidates for primary validation. Subsequent CHPP final and preceding-round checks resolved **all thirty: 27 stored winners were the losing finalists, and three stored names were correct**. The primary evidence is retained in `qa/cup-stored-primary-results.json`; it supports aggregate winners and actual opponents without matchdetails requests. The source-review JSON preserves the initial classifications alongside the resolved findings. These are pre-repair snapshots.

The seven initially corroborated winner-only records are Bulgaria 2 (Levski 1914), Bulgaria 6 (Yellow squad), Chile 9 (Universidad de Chile), Chile 11 (Sion), Croatia 4 (Folka United), Slovenia 7 (NK batman), and Scotland 4 (Barons Boys). Their public evidence and exact pre-change database rows with hashes remain in `qa/cup-stored-winner-corroboration-other.json`. All seven subsequently agreed with the primary aggregate results, so the repair plan can use the stronger evidence and does not need the winner-only fallback. Numeric team IDs and historical owner IDs remain unverified.

Corroboration includes the [Bulgarian cup winners](https://wiki.hattrick.org/wiki/Bulgarian_National_Cup), the contemporary [Yellow squad club history](https://wiki.hattrick.org/wiki/Yellow_squad), [Chilean Cup champions](https://wiki.hattrick.org/wiki/Chilean_Cup), [Croatian Cup winners](https://wiki.hattrick.org/wiki/Kup_Hrvatske), [Slovenian Cup winners](https://wiki.hattrick.org/wiki/Pokal_Slovenije), and the [Scottish Cup finals list](https://wiki.hattrick.org/wiki/Scottish_Cup). Each structured case also links the corresponding national table and states what the sources do and do not establish.

The primary aggregate results confirm the stored winners for Egypt 6 (`Alexandria Pharaons`), Iceland 10 (`Starkers`) and Iceland 12 (`Valkyries`). The wiki differs by spelling in Egypt and appears to swap the team/manager columns in those Iceland rows (`sir_mb`/`Starkers`, `Mummi`/`Valkyries`). These three stored champions remain unchanged.

After the parent applied the historical inserts and winner corrections, the cached comparison was rerun independently against the repaired database: **408 of 408 records across all 33 countries were compared, with 405 exact winner-name agreements and only those three confirmed wiki discrepancies**. The larger total includes 42 newly recovered records within this screening scope. All 27 wrong-club differences from the original comparison are gone. The original 366-row/30-difference snapshot remains unchanged, and `postRepairValidation` in the source-review JSON records this final check. No database writes or new source requests were made by the validation.

The executable comparison is `node qa/audit-stored-cup-wiki-other.mjs`. Its inputs are reviewed table facts in `qa/wiki-stored-audit/extracted-national-trophy-rows.json`; its current output is `qa/cup-stored-winner-mismatches-other.json`. The original 366-row snapshot is preserved in `qa/cup-stored-winner-mismatches-other-before-fixes.json`. The output's `mismatches` array retains the initial strict definition of a wiki winner exactly matching the stored runner-up. Reconstruction placeholders generally lack runner-up names, so an empty array does not mean all winners agreed; review `candidates` together with the source-review and corroboration files.
